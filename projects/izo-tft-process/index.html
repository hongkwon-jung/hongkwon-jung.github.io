03Troubleshooting

Process Troubleshooting

Al electrode oxidation

Situation
After the 400 ℃ anneal in air, the Al electrodes oxidized and contact resistance rose sharply
Diagnosis
Only a higher vacuum and a slow deposition differed from earlier runs; literature shows that purer Al films are weaker under annealing
Action
Deposited only the first ≈2 nm at the contact interface at a low rate, then the rest at a higher rate
Result
No recurrence

Chamber condensation in the e-beam evaporator

Situation
Time to base pressure rose from about 1.5 h to over 4 h
Diagnosis
Moisture condensed on the cold walls while the chamber was open with the chiller on
Action
Ran the chiller only during deposition
Result
Base pressure reached

Precursor moisture absorption

Situation
Film quality dropped sharply with the same recipe
Diagnosis
Traced to moisture absorption by comparing room humidity and precursor condition
Action
Dry precursor storage and RH ≤ 45%
Result
Repeatability improved

05Lessons

Lessons

  1. Recipe conditions are set at the balance point among on-current, off-current, and SS, and the process window must be rechecked whenever the composition changes
  2. A comparison of conditions holds only on a baseline process that controls equipment deviation and environmental variables
  3. A high-purity film at the deposition step can become a weakness in the next process, so a recipe must be set with the following process in mind
