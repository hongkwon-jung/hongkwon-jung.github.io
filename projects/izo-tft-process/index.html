2T1C Display Pixel Based on Solution-Processed Oxide TFTs

Chambit Design Semester · Design of solution-processed n-type oxide TFTs for next-generation displays

The goal was to drive a 2T1C display pixel with sol-gel solution-processed oxide TFTs. By establishing the process recipe and finding an additive to replace Ga, we improved the TFT mobility 2.6-fold, reduced SS to 1/5.8 of its initial value, and lowered hysteresis from 7.8 to 1.03 V. TCAD mixed-mode simulation calibrated to the measured devices confirmed 2T1C pixel operation.

01Objective

Driving a 2T1C Pixel with Solution-Processed Oxide TFTs

Switching TFTs in display pixels require high performance, so solution-processed oxides are rarely used for them. The goal of this project was to drive a 2T1C display pixel with sol-gel solution-processed oxide TFTs.

To this end, we chose In-rich IZO as the channel. It offers high mobility, but its off-current and Vth are unstable. We secured TFT performance by establishing the process recipe and optimizing an additive to replace Ga, and then confirmed 2T1C pixel operation with a TCAD model calibrated to the measured devices.

02Approach

TFT Fabrication, Process Optimization, and Reproducibility

I carried out the entire process myself, from solution synthesis to I-V measurement.

  • In a comparison of soft-bake at 130, 140, and 150 ℃, SS degraded at 130 ℃ and on-current decreased at 150 ℃, so 140 ℃ was selected. For the 10:1 composition, the bake was re-optimized and 130 ℃ in O2 was chosen because it was more stable.
  • Treating UV-O3 as a contact-treatment variable, compared treatment locations and applied it to the S/D contacts only, for 2 min. This kept the on-current loss small while improving off-current and SS and reducing device-to-device variation.
  • Limited the 400 ℃ anneal after TFT completion to 1 min. Longer annealing reduces oxygen vacancies excessively and degrades on-current.
  • Proposing the next conditions from each result, compared five additives (F, Zr, Al, Hf, Ga) and In:Zn compositions with the team and selected 10:1 IZO-F 8%.

Reproducibility

Film quality and device characteristics were not reproduced consistently even under the same conditions, so we established a baseline process before expanding the composition range.

  • Organized 32 items that could act as variables across the whole process into a process variable table and fixed them one by one
  • Fixed the solvent to 2-ME and standardized all additive recipes on a 2-ME basis
  • Measured the actual temperature deviation of each hot plate and assigned the one with the smallest deviation to soft-bake, the most temperature-sensitive step
  • Reduced film stains and coating non-uniformity by storing the In precursor dry and keeping process humidity at or below 45%

With O2 soft-bake at 140 ℃ combined with S/D UV-O3, the position-to-position variation in all four parameters was smaller than with soft-bake at 130 ℃. The hysteresis averaged over devices at nine positions on the optimized chip was 0.6 V, confirming position-independent reproducibility.

03Troubleshooting

Process Troubleshooting

Al electrode oxidation

Situation
Al films deposited late in the project had higher reflectance than usual. Characteristics were normal before annealing, but after the 400 ℃ anneal in air the electrodes oxidized and the contact resistance rose sharply.
Diagnosis
A comparison of humidity, vacuum level, current, voltage, beam focus, anneal temperature and humidity, and rate with previous depositions showed only two differences: a higher vacuum (lower pressure) than usual and a long deposition at a low rate. High reflectance is characteristic of a purer, less oxidized film, and the cause was judged from literature reporting that purer Al films are more vulnerable to deformation during annealing.
Action
Revised the deposition recipe to open the shutter only after the rate reaches a set level at the usual vacuum, and to raise the current gradually during deposition.
Result
No recurrence, and the additive comparison and 2T1C verification proceeded

Condensation in the e-beam evaporator chamber

Situation
While process-room humidity was high due to an air-conditioner failure, the base pressure, normally reached in about 1.5 hours, was not reached after more than 4 hours of pumping.
Diagnosis
Concluded that moisture condensed on the cold inner walls while the chamber was open with the chiller running, during loading of the crucible and wafers.
Action
Changed the operating sequence to run the chiller only during deposition.
Result
Base pressure reached

Precursor moisture absorption

Situation
Film quality dropped sharply with the same recipe.
Diagnosis
Narrowed the cause to moisture absorption by comparing process-room humidity with the precursor condition.
Action
Added dry precursor storage and a process-humidity limit of 45% RH to the baseline process.
Result
Repeatability under the same conditions improved, making the subsequent additive comparison reliable

4:1 optimized recipe: soft-bake at 140 ℃ in O2 with S/D UV-O3. In:Zn of 12:1 or higher did not raise the on-current because of degraded film quality, so 10:1 was chosen. Among the five additives, F 8% gave the best balance of off-current, SS, and mobility.

2T1C pixel operation

We calibrated the TCAD model to the measured TFT characteristics and simulated, in mixed mode, a 2T1C pixel circuit consisting of a switching TFT, a driving TFT, and a storage capacitor.

  • With low leakage and high on-current, even a 10 nF storage capacitor charges quickly
  • Multi-level operation, in which the drive current changes stepwise with the data voltage, confirmed control of the LED current (brightness)
  • 2T1C operation confirmed with every channel in the pixel made of solution-processed oxide
05Lessons

Lessons

  1. Recipe conditions are set at the balance point among on-current, off-current, and SS, and the process window must be rechecked whenever the composition changes
  2. A comparison of conditions holds only on a baseline process that controls equipment deviation and environmental variables
  3. A high-purity film at the deposition step can become a weakness in the next process, so a recipe must be set with the following process in mind
