01Objective

Conditions That Oxide Channels and ALD Must Meet in 3D DRAM

Planar DRAM has reached scaling limits in storage capacitance, access-transistor leakage, and refresh efficiency. This review organizes, in one flow from cell architecture to process, what oxide semiconductor channels and ALD processes must satisfy in 3D DRAM.

02Approach

Writing Scope

  • Explained why the transition to 3D is needed (capacitor, leakage, and refresh limits) and why oxide semiconductors and ALD are used (BEOL thermal budget of about 400 ℃, conformal coverage of high-aspect-ratio structures)
  • Compared 1T1C and 2T0C cell architectures and organized channel requirements by transistor role (ultralow leakage for write, high mobility for read)
  • Divided nine 3D DRAM architecture challenges into those quantitatively confirmed and those extrapolated from Si devices, ranked them by urgency, and wrote the future directions
  • Corrected missing temperature conditions and evidence mismatches by cross-checking the cited originals
03Troubleshooting

Four-Person Co-Writing Environment

Version confusion, recurring format errors, and lost comments

Situation
With four people working on one manuscript at once, it became unclear which file was the latest version, already-fixed format errors reappeared, and comments were lost
Action
Set the working conditions first, then addressed the limitations found at each stage in the next version of the environment
  • Working rules: file naming and master-copy rules, comment rules, rules for four-person simultaneous editing
  • Format unification: distributed a macro that standardizes formatting, plus a feedback tracker
  • Team website: built, with AI, a team website with task assignment, manuscript comments, a reference library, and revision history
  • Merging and verification: led manuscript merging and citation verification

Five functions of ALD in 3D cells

ALD was framed not as simple conformal deposition but as a channel process that controls thickness, composition, defects, and crystallization.

  • Conformal deposition in high-aspect-ratio structures
  • Sub-nanometer thickness control
  • Composition control by supercycles
  • Suppression of oxygen vacancies, hydrogen, and interface traps
  • Formation within the BEOL thermal budget of about 400 ℃
05Lessons

Lessons

  1. The promise of a device technology can be judged not by a single performance metric but only by matching cell architecture, channel material, and process window together
  2. When several people work on one manuscript, working conditions should be set before choosing tools, and the limitations found at each stage addressed in the next version of the environment, so that everyone works to the same standard
