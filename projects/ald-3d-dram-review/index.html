01Objective

What Oxide Channels and ALD Must Meet in 3D DRAM

Planar DRAM is reaching scaling limits in storage capacitance, access-transistor leakage, and refresh efficiency. The review sets out what oxide channels and ALD must satisfy in 3D DRAM, from cell architecture to process.

02Approach

Writing Scope

  • Why 3D (capacitor, leakage, refresh limits) and why oxide semiconductors and ALD (BEOL thermal budget of about 400 ℃, conformal coverage)
  • Channel requirements by transistor role in 1T1C and 2T0C cells
  • Nine 3D DRAM challenges, split into quantitatively confirmed and Si-extrapolated ones and ranked by urgency, plus future directions
  • Cross-checked cited originals to correct missing temperature conditions and evidence mismatches

05Lessons

Lessons

  1. The promise of a device technology can be judged not by a single performance metric but only by matching cell architecture, channel material, and process window together
  2. When several people work on one manuscript, working conditions should be set before choosing tools, and the limitations found at each stage addressed in the next version of the environment, so that everyone works to the same standard
