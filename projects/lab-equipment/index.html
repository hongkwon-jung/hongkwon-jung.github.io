02Troubleshooting

Problem, Cause, Action

E-beam cooling-water leak

Situation
Pressure did not come down at the rough-vacuum stage of the shared e-beam evaporator
Diagnosis
Water was found inside the chamber after venting, so the cooling-water path was checked first. After confirming with the vendor engineer how far a user may disassemble, opened the plate under the crucible and narrowed the cause to heat damage of the O-ring sealing the cooling-water channel
Action
Finding handed to the engineer, who replaced the O-ring
Result
Repair time shortened

E-beam vacuum leak

Situation
Reaching high vacuum took over 6 h
Diagnosis
Applied a small amount of acetone at each joint while watching the vacuum gauge, and located a poorly fastened joint between the main chamber and the rotary pump
Action
Re-fastened the joint
Result
Pump-down cut from over 6 h to about 1.5 h, and the vendor setup in the same repair raised the rate cap from 0.7 – 0.8 to 1.5 Å/s

E-beam chamber condensation

Situation
High humidity while the process-room air conditioner was out of order, over 4 h of pumping did not go below 10−5 Torr
Diagnosis
Suspected moisture on the chilled inner wall during venting and loading with the chiller on
Action
Chiller off during venting, loading and pumping, on only for deposition
Result
Normal pump-down and deposition without replacing parts

Gas measurement system re-setup

Situation
Long-unused gas system and MFCs, with toxic NO2 and NH3
Diagnosis
MoS2 reference device did not respond. Under N2 the O2-depletion alarm at the chamber stayed silent while the MFC read normal flow, narrowing the leak to between the MFC and the chamber
Action
Located the leaking fittings with leak-detection fluid (bubble test) and fixed them, corrected the line order, placed a gas detector at the fittings for toxic-gas use
Result
Reference device responded normally

E-beam beam drift

Situation
Beam position drifting during deposition regardless of input
Diagnosis
Current, voltage and sweep settings normal, narrowing the cause to hardware
Action
Power cut and the cover under the chamber removed, hardened deposition residue on the exposed wiring blown off with a low-flow N2 gun
Result
Drift no longer observed

Hot-plate temperature deviation

Situation
Different results at the same set temperature
Diagnosis
Actual temperature of each plate measured at one common setting
Action
Smallest deviation to soft-bake, plates with normal stirring to solution mixing, the rest to hard bake, organic and inorganic use separated

E-beam rate readout

Situation
Deposition rate readout fluctuating strongly
Diagnosis
Current, voltage, vacuum, crucible position, beam focus and position, and chiller temperature normal, thickness per time at the usual current and voltage matched earlier runs
Action
Judged a sensor fault and completed deposition by time and total thickness
Result
Sensor fault confirmed at a later inspection
03Lessons

Lessons

  1. Readouts are cross-checked against an independent indicator, such as thickness per time or measured temperature, before being trusted
  2. An operating sequence is changed only within what each component is for, as cooling stays on whenever the beam is on
  3. A leak check is chosen by the pressure side: acetone at joints for a chamber under vacuum, a bubble test for a pressurized gas line
  4. When a symptom points to one cause, that cause is checked first before reviewing others, as water in the chamber pointed to the cooling-water path
