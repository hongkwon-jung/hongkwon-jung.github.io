02Troubleshooting

Problem, Cause, Action

Hot-plate temperature deviation

Situation
Different results at the same set temperature
Diagnosis
Actual temperature of each plate measured at one common setting
Action
Smallest deviation to soft-bake, plates with normal stirring to solution mixing, the rest to hard bake, organic and inorganic use separated

E-beam pump-down delay

Situation
High humidity while the process-room air conditioner was out of order, over 4 h of pumping did not go below 10−5 Torr
Diagnosis
Suspected moisture on the chilled inner wall during venting and loading with the chiller on, cooling is required only while the beam is on, as the March – April O-ring failure showed
Action
Chiller off during venting, loading and pumping, on only for deposition
Result
Normal pump-down and deposition without replacing parts

E-beam rate readout

Situation
Deposition rate readout fluctuating strongly
Diagnosis
Current, voltage, vacuum, crucible position, beam focus and position, and chiller temperature normal, thickness per time at the usual current and voltage matched earlier runs
Action
Judged a sensor fault and completed deposition by time and total thickness
Result
Sensor fault confirmed at a later inspection
03Lessons

Lessons

  1. Readouts are cross-checked against an independent indicator, such as thickness per time or measured temperature, before being trusted
  2. An operating sequence is changed only within what each component is for, as cooling stays on whenever the beam is on
