Using a Sentaurus model calibrated to measurements, identified the mechanism of the Gaussian response, quantified the effects of seven structural parameters, and proposed application-specific design guidelines.

01Objective

Formation Conditions of the Gaussian Response

A Gaussian (anti-ambipolar) transistor conducts only within a specific gate-voltage window.

Goal: identify when this response forms in a p-type WSe2/n-type IGZO heterojunction and quantify the effect of each structural parameter.

ComponentReference structure
Gate / dielectricp++ Si back gate / Al2O3 90 nm
ChannelsIGZO 10 nm, WSe2 5 nm (8 layers)
ElectrodesAl (IGZO side), Pd (WSe2 side)
BiasVDS = 1 V
02Approach

Calibrated Model and Structural Parameter Analysis

As the first TCAD work in the lab, with no prior material or handover, I built the physical models from the manual and literature and calibrated them to measured single-channel WSe2 and IGZO TFTs.

03Troubleshooting

Simulation Troubleshooting and Automation

Convergence failure

Situation
Calculation stopped at +0.3 V after adding the gate leakage model
Diagnosis
Log showed the electron density collapsing to zero at a contact edge
Action
Refined only the mesh at that corner
Result
Completed up to +10 V

Simulation time

Situation
13 h per run
Diagnosis
Matrix assembly took 43× longer than solving
Action
Adjusted the iteration settings
Result
6 h per run

Mechanism verification

Situation
Peak current from SRH recombination or from carrier overlap?
Diagnosis
A 100× change in SRH lifetime changed the peak current by less than 0.03%
Action
Confirmed the conclusion holds when the interface trap density is varied
Result
Published: simultaneous n–p conduction and WSe2-side carrier overlap set the response

Fitting automation

Situation
About two months of manual fitting per model redesign
Action
Built a six-role AI pipeline: evidence, sensitivity, next condition, execution, evaluation, audit. Physical models and priority characteristics decided by me
Result
31 of 39 IGZO fitting runs converged
04Results

Response Mechanism and Design Guidelines

Mechanism

The peak current is set by simultaneous conduction of the n- and p-channels and carrier overlap on the WSe2 side, not by a single channel turning on or by the sum of two currents.

05Lessons

Lessons

  1. With many model parameters, separating those to fix from those to tune is what makes it possible to distinguish the physics governing measured characteristics from the effects of structural parameters
  2. Even with a new tool learned without reference materials, once its principles are established, the repetitive work can be automated to shorten the verification cycle
