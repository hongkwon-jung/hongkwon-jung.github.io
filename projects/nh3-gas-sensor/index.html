Room-Temperature NH3 Gas Sensor: Material Design and Sensing Characteristics

I selected MBene myself, detected 1 ppm NH3 at room temperature without heating or UV, and confirmed material-specific selectivity opposite to that of MXene on the same IZO.

01Objective

Requirements

Target: continuous NH3 monitoring at the site boundary of livestock facilities. Requirements follow Annex 3 of the Enforcement Rule of the Malodor Prevention Act (1 ppm outside industrial areas).

ItemRequirement
Detection level1 ppm NH3
Response time60 s

02Approach

Material Selection and Synthesis

  • Targeted NH3 with the OH terminations of MBene, unlike prior oxide/MBene sensors that targeted NO2 with F terminations
  • Synthesized MXene and MBene in three batches by etching route, with sedimentation conditions from Stokes' law
  • Deposited MXene and MBene on solution-processed IZO channels
03Troubleshooting

Measurement System and Reliability

System re-setup and safety

Situation
Long-unused gas system and MFCs, with toxic NO2 and NH3
Diagnosis
Set up with N2 and air first; a MoS2 reference device did not respond, pointing to gas supply
Action
Fixed the line order and loose fittings, placed a gas detector at the fittings
Result
Reference device responded normally

Substrate leakage

Situation
Large noise and unstable resistance
Diagnosis
Leakage paths of several to several hundred MΩ in substrates that should be open
Action
Substrate cleaning and screening
Result
Response separated from noise

Excluding UV

Situation
Prior studies often use UV
Diagnosis
UV lowered the resistance from 1.96 to 1.42 kΩ, burying the gas response
Action
Measured without UV

Material control

Situation
Response from MBene or from the IZO channel?
Diagnosis
30 s at 1 ppm NH3: bare IZO 0.26% resistance increase, MBene/IZO 2.03% decrease
Result
The response comes from MBene

05Lessons

Lessons

  1. A research direction can be set by starting from the requirements and the surface properties of the material
  2. Results from a new measurement system can be claimed only after separating the contributions of the equipment and the material with reference and control devices
