Room-Temperature NH3 Gas Sensor: Material Design and Sensing Characteristics

I selected MBene myself, detected 1 ppm NH3 at room temperature without heating or UV, and confirmed material-specific selectivity opposite to that of MXene on the same IZO.

01Objective

Requirements and Research Direction

Targeting a sensor that continuously monitors NH3 at the site boundary of livestock facilities, we set the requirements based on the standard in Annex 3 of the Enforcement Rule of the Malodor Prevention Act (1 ppm at the site boundary outside industrial areas).

ItemRequirement
Detection level1 ppm NH3
Response time60 s
Recovery time120 s
Operating conditionRoom temperature, no heating or UV
02Approach

Material Selection and Synthesis

  • Unlike prior oxide/MBene sensors that targeted NO2 through F terminations, set the research direction and chose the material to target NH3 through the OH terminations of MBene
  • Synthesized MXene and MBene in three batches by etching route and calculated sedimentation separation conditions with Stokes' law
  • Fabricated sensors with MXene and MBene deposited on solution-processed IZO channels
03Troubleshooting

Building the Gas Measurement System and Measurement Reliability

System re-setup and safety verification

Situation
Toxic gases such as NO2 and NH3 had to be measured with gas measurement equipment and MFCs that had not been used for a long time
Diagnosis
To prepare for leaks, set up first with N2 and air and verified with a MoS2 reference device of known response. The resistance did not change when the gas was switched, pointing to a gas-supply problem
Action
Traced the lines, corrected a connection-order error and loose fittings, placed a gas detector near those fittings, and only then used toxic gases
Result
Measurement started after confirming the normal response of the reference device

Substrate leakage path

Situation
In the first measurement, noise was large and the resistance unstable
Diagnosis
Found leakage paths of several to several hundred MΩ in substrates that should have been electrically open
Action
Secured open substrates by cleaning and screening
Result
Response separated from noise

Excluding UV illumination

Situation
Prior studies often use UV illumination as well
Diagnosis
Turning on UV lowered the resistance from 1.96 to 1.42 kΩ, burying the gas response
Action
Decided to measure without UV

Material control experiment

Situation
Needed to determine whether the NH3 response comes from MBene or from the IZO channel
Diagnosis
Under the same 30 s exposure to 1 ppm NH3, bare IZO showed a 0.26% resistance increase and MBene/IZO a 2.03% resistance decrease
Result
Confirmed that the NH3 response comes from MBene, not from the IZO channel
04Results

Room-Temperature NH3 Sensing and Selectivity

Room-temperature NH3 sensing

The theoretical detection limit is extrapolated from the linear range of 1 – 3 ppm. Within the MBene sensor literature surveyed, no report of room-temperature ppm-level NH3 detection was found.

05Lessons

Lessons

  1. A research direction can be set by starting from the requirements and the surface properties of the material
  2. Results from a new measurement system can be claimed only after separating the contributions of the equipment and the material with reference and control devices
